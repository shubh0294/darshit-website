Onion

We trade, export, wholesale and supply onion in mesh bags and jute bags, in small, medium and big sizes.

Red Onion

Grown from quality seeds and processed to international standards. Known for rich colour and taste.

  • Small: 20–30 mm, fresh, rich taste
  • Medium: 40–45+ mm, long shelf life
  • Big: 50–60+ mm, attractive colour, long shelf life

Season: mostly November to February, in all sizes and domestic or international packing.

Summer Onion

Also called pink or yellow onion. A dry onion with a strong flavour and papery, copper-hued skin over firm, creamy white flesh.

Higher in sulfur than white onion, giving a sharper, more complex taste. Suited to dishes like French onion soup. Generally available through the year.
